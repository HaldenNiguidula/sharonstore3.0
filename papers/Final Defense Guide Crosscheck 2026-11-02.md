## Evaluation instrument consistency

Visual inspection finds an additional issue beyond simple checklist compliance. The questionnaires use “Interaction Capability” and “Flexibility,” whereas the methods and summary tables use “Usability” and “Portability.” The expert questionnaire visibly contains 30 rating statements across its eight categories, while the manuscript says 29 criteria. Confirm the exact questionnaire administered and its source/version, then use a documented mapping or revise the methods and results consistently. Preserve the real research record; do not revise historical responses to fit a new instrument.

The earlier aggregation concern remains: 4.58 is the equal mean of group scores 4.66 and 4.50, not a respondent-count-weighted mean across 55 people. The guide's requested frequency/percentage appendices would help make the results auditable. Recalculate from original responses after deciding and disclosing the weighting method.

## What this guide does not establish

This two-page guide does not itself require proofreading, originality, or statistical-processing certificates, a source-code appendix, a particular abstract word limit, specific margins, APA formatting, or five recent sources in each literature category. Some of those appear in the separately supplied BSIS Research Framework. Treat them as separate institutional requirements to reconcile with the adviser, not as omissions proved by this final defense guide. The guide's appendix exceptions do not justify discarding evidence for interviews and evaluations that the manuscript claims were conducted.
